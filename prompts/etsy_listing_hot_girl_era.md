## Description

```
NOT TODAY, HORMONES: THE PERIMENOPAUSE TRACKER THAT ACTUALLY FEELS LIKE YOU 🔥

Hot Girl Era is a digital perimenopause symptom tracker and menopause journal for GoodNotes, with Margo: a woman in her late forties, red lipstick, one silver streak, zero patience for being treated like a patient.

Track the cycle that stopped behaving, the hot flashes, the 3 am wake-ups, the moods and the brain fog, and turn it into a one-page summary you can show your doctor. Undated, so you start any day. Every tab and arrow is a link. 102 matching stickers are included.

WHAT'S INSIDE (62 pages, 3:4 iPad format)
• Hyperlinked home page, "How it works" with the hot flash scale (1–5), mood and energy scale and period legend
• BUILD YOUR HOT GIRL: 8 habit streaks unlock 8 accessory stickers (sleep mask, dumbbell, shaker, ice bottle, potion, folder, fan, crown) that you paste onto Margo. A paper-doll reward system for grown women.
• CYCLE: overview for 14 cycles (start, end, length, bleed days, flow), what counts as a change, the Hot Map (a year of hot flashes and bad nights, one square per day), symptom checklist with "since when"
• DAILY LOG: 12 undated months, one row per night: period, hot flashes 1–5, night sweat, sleep hours, mood 1–5, fog, energy 1–5, notes
• HABIT GRIDS: 12 months x 8 habits (sleep, strength, protein, water, supplements, daily log, walks, no alcohol), what helped, what made it worse, rewards unlocked
• BODY: strength tracker (12 weeks), numbers you choose to track, triggers log with the usual suspects
• DOCTOR & CARE: meds and HRT log, supplements with a 14-day streak, the one-page symptom summary for your doctor, questions and answers, labs table (FSH, estradiol, thyroid, vitamin D, ferritin and more), screening checklist and appointments
• MIND: 6 journal prompts, the rage page ("scribble, nobody is reading this"), wins, "done with / into now", energy audit
• 7 notes pages (lined, dotted, grid)
• PDF bookmarks for the GoodNotes outline

102 STICKERS INCLUDED
• 24 Margo moments: fanning herself, hugging ice water, opening the window in winter, fighting the thermostat, brain fog, lifting, 3 am scrolling, dancing in the kitchen
• 30 symptom and care labels (hot flash, night sweat, brain fog, period, spotting, HRT, labs, water, protein, win)
• Build your Hot Girl: base Margo and the 8 rewards
• Hot flash thermometers 1–5, moons and drops for the cycle, ice, thermostat, open window
• 12 little quotes: "not today, hormones", "it's not me, it's estrogen", "main character, still", "open the window", "fog mode: on", "still hot. literally."
• Washi, frame, banner, arrow, heart, lipstick, cat-eye sunglasses, iced coffee, cat, flame
• Transparent PNG with a die-cut edge, plus a sticker book PDF to lasso and paste

FILES YOU GET
• Hot_Girl_Era_Perimenopause_Planner.pdf (the tracker)
• Hot_Girl_Era_Stickers_Part1.zip and Part2.zip (transparent PNG, sorted in folders)
• Hot_Girl_Era_Sticker_Book.pdf
• How_to_import_stickers.pdf (Penly, Samsung Notes, Xodo, GoodNotes steps)

HOW TO USE
1. Download the PDF and import it into GoodNotes (Import > choose file).
2. Add the PNG stickers to GoodNotes Elements, or open the sticker book and lasso-copy.
3. Every evening: one row in the daily log. Thirty seconds, no essays.

ON ANDROID (Penly, Samsung Notes, Xodo, Noteshelf)
• Unzip the sticker file in My Files. Penly: + > Stickers > "Create category from folder". Samsung Notes: Image > From Gallery. Xodo / Noteshelf: Insert > Image.
• A one-page import guide is inside the zip.

GOOD TO KNOW
• This is a tracking tool, not medical advice. It is built to make your conversation with a doctor easier, not to replace it.
• Instant digital download, nothing is shipped.
• Works with GoodNotes 5 and 6, Notability, Penly, Samsung Notes, Xodo, Noteshelf and other PDF apps that support hyperlinks. iPad and Android tablets.
• Undated: start today, in any month.
```
