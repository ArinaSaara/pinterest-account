## Description

```
HATCH A DRAGON FROM YOUR HABITS 🐉

Meet Ember, Lumi, Marin and Volt. Four tiny dragons, four seasons, one promise: show up for your habits and they grow. Keep a streak, and the egg cracks. Keep going, and a baby dragon curls up on your page. By the end of the season, an antlered adult moves into your Hall of Dragons, and you have a tiny trophy shelf of everything you did this year.

No streak? Nothing breaks. The dragon simply waits. This is a planner that cheers, not one that nags.

Here is how it works:
• Each month page has 5 habits and a growth bar with 8 faded dragon stages.
• Hit a streak milestone (3, 7, 10 days...), lasso the next stage from the sticker sheet, paste it over the faded one.
• Watch your dragon grow from egg to adult, season by season.

Four seasons, four dragons, four life areas:
• Autumn · Ember the fire dragon · energy, movement and daily habits
• Winter · Lumi the ice dragon · rest, reading and sleep
• Spring · Marin the water dragon · hydration, mood and self-care
• Summer · Volt the lightning dragon · study, projects and savings

WHAT'S INSIDE (33 pages, 1536 x 2048 px, iPad 3:4)
• Cover and hyperlinked contents: tap a season, a month or a tracker and jump straight there
• How it works page with the growth mechanic
• Hall of Dragons: collect all four adults
• 4 season covers
• 4 sticker sheets with all 8 life stages of each dragon
• 12 monthly habit trackers (5 habits x every day of the month) with a growth bar
• 8 themed trackers: quarterly habits, focus sessions, reading log, sleep log, water intake, mood, study sessions, dragon hoard savings
• Blank dotted notes page
• Bookmarks in the PDF sidebar; every inner page title links back to contents

STICKERS
• 32 transparent PNG stickers (4 dragons x 8 stages: egg, hatchling, baby, child, juvenile, adolescent, young adult, adult)
• Die-cut watercolor style, 1000 x 1500 px, ready to drop into GoodNotes Elements
• 4 sticker sheets as PNG if you prefer lasso and copy

HOW TO USE
1. Download the PDF and import it into GoodNotes (Import > choose file).
2. Add the transparent PNGs to GoodNotes Elements, or keep the sticker sheets in the planner and lasso what you need.
3. Fill a month, track your streak, paste the next dragon stage when you reach a milestone.

FILES YOU GET
• Year_of_Dragons_Planner.pdf (full planner)
• Stickers_Fire_Ice.zip and Stickers_Water_Lightning.zip (transparent PNG)
• Quick start guide PDF

GOOD TO KNOW
• Instant digital download, no physical item will be shipped.
• Undated: the calendar grid follows a generic month, so you can start any year.
• Works on iPad with GoodNotes 5 and 6, Notability, Samsung Notes, Xodo and other PDF apps. Hyperlinks need an app that supports them.
• Personal use only. Please do not resell, share or redistribute the files or the artwork.
• The dragon artwork was created with AI image tools and refined by hand; the planner layout, trackers and mechanics are my own design.
• Because this is a digital product, returns are not possible. If something does not work, message me and I will help.

Looking for one season only? The Fire, Ice, Water and Lightning Season Planners are available separately in my shop.
```
